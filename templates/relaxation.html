Aromatherapy Guide

Essential oils for mind and body wellness

💜 Lavender (Calming & Sleep)

Benefits: Eases anxiety, lowers heart rate, and promotes deep, restful sleep.

Health Tip: Place 2-3 drops of lavender oil on your pillow, or diffuse it in your bedroom 30 minutes before sleep.
💚 Peppermint (Energy & Focus)

Benefits: Eases tension headaches, reduces fatigue, and sharpens concentration.

Health Tip: Inhale peppermint oil vapor directly from the bottle to boost morning alertness.
💙 Eucalyptus (Respiratory Care)

Benefits: Clears sinuses, supports respiratory health, and has natural antimicrobial properties.

Health Tip: Add 3-5 drops to your shower floor for steam inhalation.
💛 Chamomile (Stress & Digestion)

Benefits: Reduces mood swings, calms sensitive skin, and helps settle an upset stomach.

Health Tip: Combine diffusion with a warm cup of chamomile tea for powerful relaxation.
🤍 Tea Tree (Antiseptic & Immunity)

Benefits: Strong antiseptic properties, helps treat skin irritations/acne, and boosts immune response.

Health Tip: Always dilute with carrier oil in a 1:10 ratio before applying to skin.
🧡 Lemon (Mood & Detoxification)

Benefits: Energizing, helps purify indoor air quality, and supports lymphatic drainage.

Health Tip: Diffuse in your kitchen or workspace in the morning for an uplifting atmosphere.

Critical Safety Tips

  • Always Dilute: Never apply essential oils directly to the skin. Dilute in carrier oils.
  • Patch Test: Test a single diluted drop on your forearm for 24 hours first.
  • Never Ingest: Essential oils are toxic when swallowed.
  • Pet Safety: Several oils are toxic to dogs and cats. Diffuse in well-ventilated areas.
